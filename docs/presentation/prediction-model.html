PassionLab · interest inference

How the number is made

Everything that goes into deciding a child has a spike, from a single click on a screen to a candidate a guide is allowed to promote. Every constant, definition and formula below is read from the running engine, and every worked figure was computed by it.

Beta-Bernoulli, one cell at a time Engine · @gt100k/interest-inference Signals · @gt100k/signal-pipeline
00 · THE SHAPE OF IT

Six stages, and the last one is a person.

01 rawInteractionA child touches something. Who, what, when, and whether we nudged them there.
02 classifyEvent kindOne of eleven kinds. Three of them can never score.
03 weightEvidence for and againstEach kind carries a fixed weight, aged by how long ago it happened.
04 foldα and β per cellA cell is one subject crossed with one way of working.
05 posteriorMean, spread, lower boundWe report the pessimistic edge, not the average.
06 gateCandidateFour conditions at once, then a named human decides.
A cell is the unit of belief. Not a subject, and not a child. It is one (domain × mode) pair, for example Music & Sound × build or Math & Puzzles × investigate. The same child holds a separate belief for every cell they have touched, which is how the system can say a child is drawn to the subject, the way of working, or both.
01 · DEFINITIONS

What each event kind actually means.

These are the exact rules, not paraphrases. There is no voluntary return event kind, because the concept was too vague to score: it once covered both "reopened it thirty seconds later" and "came back on day four unprompted", which are not the same claim. The two kinds below replaced it. voluntary survives only as a flag on the raw interaction, meaning "we did not nudge them".

Two rules keep the negatives honest. A session in which the child neither engaged nor opened anything produces no skips and no declines, because a child who could not find a way in is not a child who rejected everything. And merely opening something counts as taking it, not as passing it over, so a child who glances and leaves does not earn a decrement against the one cell they demonstrably chose to look at.
02 · THE CHOICE SET

One choice moment is worth one skip, however many things were on screen.

choiceSetSize is the number of cells that were offered, not taken, and past their novelty window. Each one's weight is divided by it, so the six alternatives you passed over to pick a seventh share a single skip's worth of disconfirming mass between them rather than each taking a full one. Without this, a child who returns to their favourite cell every session accrues a full decrement against every other known cell every session, and breadth of discovery would suppress the very spike the engine exists to find.

03 · WHERE A CELL STARTS

A coin flip, nudged by circumstance.

Before a child does anything, every cell sits at even odds. Three facts about the child's world can tilt the starting point, and only upward.

α₀ = 1  +  0.5 if the domain exists in their environment
   +  0.5 × masteryTilt  // how they already do in it at school, [0,1]
   +  0.5 × discretionaryTilt  // free-choice time they already give it, [0,1]
β₀ = 1  // nothing tilts the against side. There is no prior reason to disbelieve.

A prior can never make a cell confident. The sufficiency gates in stage 06 count only evidence recorded after the prior, and the day gate counts only days the child acted, so the most favourable prior in the world still leaves a cell uncertain until the child has actually done something on separate days. This is also why morning academic data can seed an afternoon prior without ever gating what a child may explore.
04 · RECENCY

Everything halves every fourteen days.

Two masses, because decay answers the wrong question twice. Decay is geometric, so a steady cadence converges: fortnightly returns ceiling at a decayed mass of 2.0 against a sufficiency floor of 6, meaning a child who came back every other week for years could never be confident at any number of visits. So the engine keeps both.

α and β carry the decayed weights and answer what do we believe now. observedMass sums the same weights undecayed and answers have we seen enough to say anything at all. Observing does not un-happen because time passed.
05 · THE POSTERIOR

The whole posterior, in seven lines.

α = α₀ + Σ ( weight × roleScale × r(t) )  // returns and depth
   + Σ ( 0.25 × r(t) )  // broad reports; they carry no role
β = β₀ + Σ ( weight × r(t) ÷ max(1, choiceSetSize) )  // skips and declines

n = α + β
mean = α ÷ n
variance = (α × β) ÷ ( n × n × (n + 1) )  // Beta distribution variance
sd = √variance
lowerBound = max( 0 , mean − 1.0 × sd )  // the number everything downstream reads

Why the lower bound and not the mean

A cell with two events and a cell with twenty can share a mean of 0.85. Subtracting a standard deviation separates them, because the thin one has a wider spread and loses more. The system under-claims by construction, and the penalty falls hardest on exactly the evidence that deserves least trust.

What it is not

This is a deliberately cheap conservative point estimate, not a Beta credible interval. A proper quantile is the obvious upgrade and is not built. Said plainly here so nobody has to discover it under questioning.

roleScale is the only remaining multiplier, and it is 0.5 for the secondary reading of a two-mode action, where the mode was afforded rather than directly acted. It exists as a named engine constant rather than a number on the event, because the field it replaced was free-numeric and an emitter could have filled it with active time.
06 · THE GATES

Four conditions, all at once, then a human.

A cell becomes a promotable candidate only when every one of these holds. Three of them govern whether the engine is allowed to have an opinion; the fourth is the opinion itself.

observedMass ≥ 6Undecayed. Roughly a week of coming back, because at most one return banks per day.
distinctDays ≥ 2Distinct UTC days that moved the belief. A count can be run up in one sitting; a calendar cannot.
2 × sd ≤ 0.35The band has to be narrow enough to be worth saying out loud.
lowerBound ≥ 0.6A cell with no evidence sits at 0.5. This is meaningfully better than knowing nothing.
Only three kinds of thing can mark a day: a cross-day return, a scoring depth family, or a skip or decline. A prompted return, a same-day engagement, a novelty event and an adult report all leave distinctDays where they found it.

The adult report is the pointed one. It does count toward the mass floor, and it never marks a day, so no quantity of parent reports can make a cell confident on its own. A child must have acted on at least two separate days before anyone is allowed an opinion.

After the gates

Passing cells are ranked by lower bound and the top 3 are surfaced. Each is attributed to domain, style or mixed by comparing the evidence-weighted marginal across that subject against the marginal across that way of working, with a margin of 0.1. None of this promotes anything. Promotion is a separate human action behind three further checks.

08 · WHAT NEVER REACHES THE NUMBER

The exclusions are the design.

Most of what a system like this could measure is noise, and some of it is actively misleading. Each of these is recorded, most are shown to a guide, and none of them can move a belief.

ExcludedWhyHow the exclusion is enforced
Time on task Dwell is non-monotonic in interest. Stronger performers stay as progress rises, weaker ones stay with the most familiar option. Same number, opposite meaning. At ages 7 to 8, in-session engagement failed to separate an intrinsically motivating task from a boring one; only a delayed test did. Captured as one of four ordinal buckets on the raw interaction, and deliberately absent from the event type the belief math sees. A closed enum cannot be multiplied into α.
Attempts and success rate A child who loves a thing they are bad at must read exactly as interested as one who finds it easy. Lives on the raw interaction for the wellbeing engine. Never copied onto the event type.
Work quality Competent work in a boring subject is common, and so is poor work in a beloved one. artifact_competence is filtered out of the interest-scoring depth set by name.
Position on screen People pick the top of a list far more often than the bottom whatever is in it. Recorded at surfacing time and otherwise unused, because the size of the effect cannot be known before the surface exists. The wall also randomises order per session.
Anything the child says they like Self-report predicts behaviour only modestly, and at these ages almost every child says yes to almost every question. There is no such input.
Any reward for exploring Attach one and every child explores, so the signal measures prize-chasing. For children who already had the interest, an expected tangible reward can crowd it out. No streaks, badges, points, notifications or unlocks exist on the discovery surface.
The structural guarantee. Duration, attempts and dwell all live on the raw interaction type. The belief math only ever receives the event type. Keeping these fields off that type makes it impossible for performance or time to become interest evidence, which is a stronger promise than a comment asking people not to do it.

These are priors, not fitted parameters. They were chosen from the literature and from arguments recorded next to each one in the source. Nobody has calibrated them against a real cohort, because no real child has used this. 10 · AND THEN IT STOPS

The engine never promotes anything.

A candidate is the most the arithmetic is allowed to produce. Turning one into a specialization a child actually pursues requires three further checks and a named adult who clicks.

Survived a gapTwo consecutive returns at least 14 days apart. A real quiet spell they came back from.
DurableTwo review cycles spanning at least 56 days.
Made somethingAn artifact that survived a setback.
A personWho also confirms the child wants it, and whose name is on it.
Nothing demotes on silence. A quiet month is not evidence of not caring. What moves a hypothesis down is contesting evidence, and unresolved family pressure holds promotion for that child entirely until a guide reviews it.
